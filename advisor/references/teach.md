# Verb: teach

Explain one judgment from the four moves to a named audience. Load
`evidence` and cite the heuristic behind the judgment by its source. When
the audience is unnamed, take student.

| Audience | Calibration |
| --- | --- |
| student | The move as a procedure they run next time, with the artifact's own text as the worked example |
| collaborator | The judgment and its evidence; the procedure is skipped |
| reviewer or funder | The claim, the instrument, the kill test; nothing about the lab's envelope |

Output: the explanation, then one line naming the heuristic and its source
from `evidence`. Nothing after it.

## Completion Checks

<checklist for="verb">
  <item>One judgment was explained, at the named audience's calibration.</item>
  <item>The closing line names a heuristic and its source from evidence.</item>
</checklist>
